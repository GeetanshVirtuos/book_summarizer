 Title: The New You Business: Transforming Customers for Lasting Success

In the modern competitive landscape, businesses must focus not only on quality, convenience, and 
experience but also on transforming their customers to achieve significant change. This approach is 
termed "The New You" Business, where companies partner with consumers to improve some fundamental 
aspect of their lives, leading to a "new you."

Key Elements of Transformations:
- Selling a distinct economic offering that goes beyond goods, services, and experiences, extending to 
complete solutions aimed at transforming customers.
- Focusing on the customer's success rather than just the products or services offered. This means 
understanding each customer's definition of success and providing everything they need to achieve their 
goals.
- Adopting a solutions mindset, where companies offer holistic solutions that cater to all aspects of 
the transformation process, rather than leaving parts for customers to figure out on their own.

Designing a Transformation Offering:
1. Ascertain the jobs to be done: Understand what customers want to achieve, categorizing their goals 
into functional, emotional, social, and aspirational jobs.
2. Define success along the way: Companies must spend time interacting with customers to understand 
what success looks like at every point in the transformation journey.
3. Identify barriers: Once a company knows what success means for a customer, they can then identify 
the obstacles that need to be overcome and figure out how to assist in overcoming them.

Case Study: TrueConnect
TrueConnect is a financial benefit provider that helps people with poor credit scores obtain loans they 
can afford to repay. By understanding their customers' jobs to be done, pain points, and barriers, 
TrueConnect was able to create a responsible alternative to payday loans. They offer loans through 
employers, provide loans quickly without credit checks, limit loan amounts, charge no fees, and offer 
free counseling on topics such as creating and sticking to a budget.

Designing the Offering:
1. Integrate solutions: Transformations involve journeys that cut across many offerings, so companies 
must integrate the goods, services, and experiences required to help customers achieve success. This 
can be done by partnering with others or offering integrated care models.
2. Engage each customer as an essential partner: Understand what expectations, know-how, and motivation 
customers need at every stage of their individual journeys. Provide support that is customized 
according to each person's unique situation, preferences, motivation, and skills.
3. Provide customized support: Transformation journeys are individual experiences; success often 
depends on customized support and guidance based on each person's circumstances. Use various means to 
provide assistance at scale.
4. Support the full range of jobs to be done: Companies should offer support for all four categories of 
jobs to be done, as demonstrated by Resolve Solutions Incorporated (RSI) which helps youths achieve 
their career aspirations.
5. Charge for outcomes: A true transformation business goes beyond charging for physical goods, company 
activities, and memorable events; it aligns its fees with what customers value—transformational 
outcomes. Examples include pay-for-success contracts in social services and outcome-based reimbursement 
models in healthcare.

Competing on transformations makes a company responsible for working with customers to define the 
transformation each one seeks, identify the barriers to success, and orchestrate all the goods, 
services, and experiences needed to support them during their journeys. This approach not only promises 
profits but also generates knowledge that the company has truly made a profound difference in its 
customers' lives.
