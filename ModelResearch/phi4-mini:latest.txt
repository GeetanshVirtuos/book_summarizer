**Summary**

The "New You" business concept discussed in this article from Harvard Business Review by Lance A. 
Bettencourt, B. Joseph Pine II, James H. Gilmore and David W. Norton focuses on creating economic value 
through transformational partnerships with consumers rather than just selling goods or services.

Key Points:

- Traditional industries like fitness centers, medical providers, colleges often focus more on the 
quality of their offerings instead of understanding consumer aspirations.
  
- Transformation businesses are unique as they help customers achieve major change beyond functional 
jobs (task-oriented goals) to emotional and aspirational ones. These include feeling empowered; wanting 
financial security or a successful career.

- Companies need complete solutions for true transformations, not just parts like diet plans but 
comprehensive programs that address the customer's holistic journey towards success.
  
- To design effective transformation offerings:
  - Integrate multiple resources required by customers
  - Engage as essential partners with individual customer journeys 
  - Provide customized support according to each person's unique situation and motivation. This can 
include predictive analytics for at-risk students or specialized mentoring programs like RSI's work in 
helping underrepresented youth.
  
- Transformation businesses promise handsome rewards not only profits but profound differences made on 
customers' lives.

**Case Studies:**

1. TrueConnect transformed the lending process by offering loans to individuals with poor credit 
scores, without regard to their history and providing a streamlined application through employer 
partnerships

2. TCU's comprehensive collaborative care model (CCCM) integrated mental health resources both within 
campus-based services as well as external outpatient behavioral-health treatment centers.

3. Noom adopted an innovative approach in helping customers lose weight by focusing on the 'why' behind 
individual goals, changing thought processes and behaviors through daily lessons or coaching according 
to their circumstances.
